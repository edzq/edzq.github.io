Most of what I learn in a week never makes it into a paper. A failed ablation, a
subtlety in how a benchmark is scored, a paragraph from someone else's related
work section that reframed a problem for me — all of it lives in scattered notes
that I cannot find again six months later.

So this is the place for it. Short pieces, written mainly for the version of me
who will need them later:

- **Reading notes.** Papers I found genuinely useful, and why.
- **Research notes.** Things that did not work, and what I think the reason was.
- **Engineering notes.** The unglamorous parts — data pipelines, evaluation
  harnesses, and the bugs that cost me a week.

Nothing here is meant to be finished or authoritative. If I get something wrong,
[tell me](mailto:qi.zhang@temple.edu).
